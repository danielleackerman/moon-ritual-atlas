System rules

What carries across all thirteen pages.

Structure remains dependable.

Page identity, source foundation, materials, threshold, true card count, closing declaration, integration, and back/next/index navigation.

Each ritual owns one mechanism.

Vellum belongs to The Invisible Fabric. Mirrors, keys, threads, signals, ledgers, and constellations receive their own material behavior inside the shared shell.

Motion follows meaning.

Layers lift, signal travels, and gold appears at completed thresholds. Reduced motion keeps every state change immediate and fully legible.

Mobile becomes sequential.

The rail condenses to seven numbered tabs, hero metadata stacks cleanly, vellum remains operable, and controls keep full 44-pixel targets.

Journal entries stay private.

Inputs use explicit labels, visible focus, readable contrast, and local persistence only if the implementation deliberately adds it.

Gold stays consequential.

It is reserved for active progress, recovered truth, movement thresholds, and the final artifact—not headings, borders, and ornament everywhere.
