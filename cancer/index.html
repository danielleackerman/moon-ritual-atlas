12 Rooms

Cancer is the first water sign and a cardinal sign — the force that begins a season through feeling. Cancer is often reduced to comfort and tenderness. But Cancer is not passive. Its symbol, the crab, does not approach the world in a straight line. It carries its protection with it, and knows when the tide has changed.

This New Moon gathers near Mercury retrograde in Cancer. Less a clean page than a house revisited at night. Old messages return. Words once swallowed ask for form. Something lost may be found — not an object, but an appetite, a voice, a boundary.

Return before beginning. Feel before defining. Receive before forcing. Build the new story from the inner house outward.
